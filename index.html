My Projects

MyBoard

Personal Knowledge Management & Whiteboarding Application

A desktop whiteboarding application for organizing reference images, notes, and ideas on an infinite canvas.
Built after running into the limitations of existing free-tier tools, it supports nested boards for structuring large amounts of information,
rich text notes, and a customizable color system.

Designed and developed independently. In active use and ongoing development.

Anito

A Roguelike Game Inspired by Philippine Mythology

A pixel-art roguelike built by a five-person team, centered on Filipino folklore.
Contribution focused on visual asset production — character, enemy, and tileset art in Aseprite — along with minor gameplay scripting in Unity.
The project was developed as a research study, incorporating survey and playtest data from 50 participants to evaluate design decisions.

UrbanGrow

Plant Suitability Recommendation Using Machine Learning-Based Light Condition Classification

A system that classifies a photo of a planting environment by light condition and recommends suitable plants accordingly.
A MobileNetV2 model was trained on 2,743 labeled images across five light categories, achieving 85.19% test accuracy.
Includes a full data pipeline and a rule-based recommendation layer, documented in an independent research paper.
